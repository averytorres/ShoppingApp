THE PATTERNS TAKING SHAPE

How often you restock

Typical time between purchases, not a fixed schedule. Each group shows whether its items have regular or variable timing.

How these groups work

For items bought in the selected period, we use up to 12 recent gaps from all available history. The middle gap sets the frequency group; consistency is assessed separately. Every few days: 1–4 days · weekly: 5–10 · every 2 weeks: 11–20 · monthly: 21–45 · every few months: 46–120 · less often: over 120. Groups use rounded days.

These are observed habits, not a fixed schedule. Old habits may no longer apply.

Purchase history sync

Every Monday at 2 a.m. Eastern time, for both stores on your default account. If this PC is off, it catches up when the server starts. Already verified receipts are skipped. Chrome keeps your store sign-ins.

Product photos

Photos are saved on this PC and shared with your household devices. The helper learns photos as you browse store product pages, search results and carts. Collect a batch for your selected store to fill gaps now.

Needs review

Compare proposed alternatives side by side, then approve a choice, change it, or mark it unsuitable. Each decision applies only to this product pairing; existing matches stay intact.

Match products across stores

Match products you would buy in place of each other, such as your usual Walmart milk and H-E-B milk. Purchases from either store then count toward the same household item. Check type and package size first.

Add or edit a household item

For package counts, set equivalent amounts: a gallon = 1, a half gallon = 0.5. Weight matches retain their units. When linking a package to a weighed product, enter grams per package (12 oz = 340.194 g).
